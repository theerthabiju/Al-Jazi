FIRE SUPPRESSION CONTROL PANEL

This fire suppression control panel is designed to detect and Respond to fire hazards in critical environments such as electrical Panels, server rooms, and industrial installations. It continuously monitors connected sensors and, upon detecting Abnormal heat or fire conditions, activates alarms and triggers The suppression system automatically. The system is built for High reliability, with organized internal components, secure wiring, and fail-safe operation to protect valuable Equipment while minimizing damage and downtime

PRI-SAFETY INDIRECTLY AUTOMATIC FIRE SUPPRESSION SYSTEM FOR SCHOOL BUS

This system automatically detects and suppresses fires in school bus engine compartments using a heat-sensitive tube. It operates without electricity and releases a wet chemical agent directly onto the fire, ensuring quick response and improved passenger safety

AN AUTOMATIC FIRE DETECTION AND SUPPRESSION SYSTEM FOR POWER TRANSFORMERS

Designed to quickly identify fire hazards—such as oil leaks, overheating, or arc faults—and suppress them before they cause major damage. The system uses heat, flame, or smoke detectors to continuously Monitor the transformer area and automatically activates suppression Methods like high-velocity water spray, foam, or clean agents. This rapid response helps minimize fire spread, protect critical equipment, ensure personnel safety, and reduce downtime in power substations.

DELUGE SYSTEMS

A deluge system is a fixed fire protection system in which all sprinklers or nozzles are open, and water is released simultaneously when the system is activated. It is commonly used in high-hazard areas such as power plants, transformers, chemical facilities, and storage tanks, where rapid fire spread is possible. Deluge systems provide fast, large-volume water discharge to quickly control or suppress fires.
