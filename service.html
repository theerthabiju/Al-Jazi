Services that We’re providing

We offer a complete range of fire safety, security, and protection services designed to meet international safety standards and regulatory requirements. Our services include system design, supply, installation, programming, testing, commissioning, and maintenance of fire fighting systems, fire alarms, suppression systems, CCTV, access control, and emergency safety solutions. Each service is delivered by qualified professionals to ensure reliability, efficiency, and long-term performance across residential, commercial, and industrial environments. With a strong focus on safety, quality, and innovation, we provide end-to-end solutions to safeguard lives, property, and critical assets.

FIRE FIGHTING SYSTEMS

We design, supply, and install complete fire fighting systems including sprinklers, fire pumps, hose cabinets, valves, and accessories. All systems meet UL, FM, and SASO standards to ensure reliable fire protection.

FIRE ALARM PROGRAMMING & SERVICES

We provide fire alarm programming, testing, commissioning, and maintenance services to ensure accurate detection and system reliability. Our AMC services support long-term performance and regulatory compliance.

FIRE SUPPRESSION SYSTEMS

We deliver advanced fire suppression solutions such as FM-200, Novec 1230, CO₂ systems, foam systems, automatic tube systems, and kitchen hood suppression for effective fire control.

BARRIERS & ENTRANCE CONTROL

We supply and install arm barriers, boom barriers, and flap barriers to manage vehicle and pedestrian access while enhancing security and smooth traffic flow.

SECURITY & LOW CURRENT SYSTEM

We offer integrated security solutions including CCTV surveillance, access control, biometric systems, and low current systems for continuous monitoring and controlled access.

SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

We provide first aid kits, safety certifications, system efficiency reports, and Taqrir Fanni technical reports to meet safety and regulatory requirements.

SAFETY & SMOKE CONTROL SYSTEMS

We install fire dampers, smoke exhaust systems, emergency lighting, and exit signage to support safe evacuation and smoke management during emergencies.

ELECTRICAL & CONTROL SYSTEMS

We deliver complete electrical and control solutions including contactors, starters, switches, gauges, control panels, and automation components for reliable system operation.
